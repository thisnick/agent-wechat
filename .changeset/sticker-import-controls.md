**Add to Saved** now lets you choose each sticker's size before adding it. Every file shows its prepared dimensions, file size and length, and a **Size** slider shrinks it: images start at their original size (anything over WeChat's 1024-pixel limit starts at 1024), videos at 240 pixels. A mark on the slider shows about 384 pixels, the largest size phones display stickers at. Videos also get **Smoothness** (frames per second), **Start** and **Length**. The preview is prepared again shortly after you stop moving a control, earlier settings come back instantly, and a result too large or too long to send is explained in plain words. **Add** waits until every selected file matches its controls.
