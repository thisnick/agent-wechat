Add direct and group voice and video calling to the client UI. Conversations now expose voice and video call triggers in the chat header and details panel, an anchored pre-call camera preview popover, a group member call picker, an in-chat active conference join bar, a non-blocking incoming call toast with a looping synthesized ringtone, a docked voice strip, draggable picture-in-picture, and a Theater and full-screen video stage with an in-call chat and participant drawer. Also adds ticket-authenticated WebSocket upgrade proxying in the preview launcher (`connect.mjs`) and visual review scenes covering the calling screens.
