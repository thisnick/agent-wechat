On macOS, message notifications are grouped by chat in Notification Center and offer **Mark as Read** and **Reply**. Mark as Read marks the chat read unless a newer message has arrived since. Reply sends your text as a quote reply to that message and marks the chat read; it appears only when the message can be quoted. Opening a chat clears its notifications, and a notification still opens its chat after the app restarts. The first notification after updating may ask again to allow notifications.
