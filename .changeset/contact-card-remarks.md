Contact cards show and edit your remark for a friend. The card names the contact by the remark, with their nickname and WeChat ID below it. Saving or clearing a remark uses the same inspected version as privacy edits and shows the new name while WeChat confirms it. Editing a remark needs account administrator access. Saved privacy and remark changes no longer show a status banner; the controls show the saved values.
